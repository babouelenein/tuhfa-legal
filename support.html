Support

Questions, problems or feedback: abouelenein.baz@gmail.com. Your email address goes with any message you send. Please don't include health details unless you choose to.

Do I need an account?

No. Every free feature works without one. An account (Sign in with Apple) is needed only for cloud backup. Tuhfa Plus can be bought and restored without one.

Which schools does Tuhfa follow?

Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali. Choose yours during set-up, or change it later in Settings. Cross-checked against the fiqh references listed in Sources and method. Currently under scholarly review. Tuhfa does not replace a scholar; when a case needs one, the app says so.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Settings > Tuhfa Plus > Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel Tuhfa Plus?

In your Apple Account settings: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tuhfa Plus. Access continues to the end of the paid period, and nothing on your phone is deleted.

I forgot my backup passphrase.

Your backup is encrypted with your passphrase on your phone before it is uploaded, so no one, including us, can recover it without the passphrase. If you still have your phone, your records on it are unaffected, and you can start a new backup from Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Settings > Privacy and data > Delete all app data from this device erases everything on the phone. Settings > Account > Delete cloud account deletes your account and every backup. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

Something looks wrong in a result.

Tap "Why this result?" on Today to see the reasoning and sources, then use "Seems wrong?" to prepare a summary for a scholar. You can also write to us at the address above. In Tuhfa 26.1 and later, Settings > About and help > Report a problem with a result opens a message in your mail app, which you send yourself. Your email address goes with it. Your summary (today's result, its reasons and your madhhab; the reasons can mention a pregnancy, a birth or a pregnancy loss) is added only if you turn on Add my summary.

Family profiles

In Tuhfa 26.1 and later, family profiles are for other adults who use the same phone: Settings > Records and planning > Family profiles. Creating one needs Tuhfa Plus with a paid subscription or Lifetime; the free trial does not include family profiles. Tuhfa Plus belongs to the main profile, so its features are not active inside the other profiles.

Where is the privacy policy in the app?

In Tuhfa 26.1 and later: Settings > Privacy and data > Privacy policy, and on the Tuhfa Plus screen next to Terms of Use.
