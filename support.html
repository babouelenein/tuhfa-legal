Do I need an account?

No. Every free feature works without one. An account (Sign in with Apple) is needed only for Tuhfa Plus and cloud backup.

Which schools does Tuhfa follow?

Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali. Choose yours during set-up, or change it later in Settings. Each ruleset has been cross-checked against the fiqh references listed in the app and is currently under scholarly review. Tuhfa does not replace a scholar; when a case needs one, the app says so.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Settings > Account, backup and subscription > Tuhfa Plus > Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel Tuhfa Plus?

In your Apple Account settings: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tuhfa Plus. Access continues to the end of the paid period, and nothing on your phone is deleted.

I forgot my backup passphrase.

Your backup is encrypted with your passphrase on your phone before it is uploaded, so no one, including us, can recover it without the passphrase. If you still have your phone, your records on it are unaffected, and you can start a new backup from Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Settings > Delete all app data from this device erases everything on the phone. Settings > Account, backup and subscription > Delete cloud account deletes your account and every backup. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

Something looks wrong in a result.

Tap "Why this result?" on Today to see the reasoning and sources, then use "Seems wrong?" to prepare a summary for a scholar. You can also write to us at the address above.
