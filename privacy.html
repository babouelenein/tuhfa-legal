Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Publisher: Baz Abouelenein · Contact: abouelenein.baz@gmail.com

The short version

Tuhfa keeps your records on your phone, in an encrypted database. You can use every free feature, and buy or restore Tuhfa Plus, without an account; an account is needed only for cloud backup. Nothing about your cycle, prayers, fasts or notes is sent to us in a form we can read, unless you write to us and include it yourself. We do not show ads, sell data or track you across apps and websites.

What stays on your phone

Your cycle, discharge, postpartum, prayer, fasting and Qada records, your notes, your school and settings, and the prayer location you choose (rounded to about 1 km) are stored in an encrypted database on your phone. The key is kept in the iOS Keychain. Prayer times are calculated on your phone.

What leaves your phone, and only if you choose it

Cloud account (optional)

If you sign in with Apple, we receive a sign-in token from Apple that holds an identifier Apple creates for your use of this app. We do not ask Apple for your name or email. Our backend provider, Supabase, creates an account identifier for you.

Encrypted backup (Tuhfa Plus)

Before a backup leaves your phone, your records are encrypted on the phone with a random key, and that key is locked with a recovery passphrase that only you know. We store the encrypted file and basic details about it (your account identifier, its number, when it was made, its size and checksums). The file also holds your backup key, locked with your passphrase. We cannot read its contents, and we cannot recover it if you lose both your passphrase and your phone.

Subscription

Purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat, our purchase provider, receives your purchase history from Apple, together with your account identifier if you are signed in or a random identifier if you are not, so that the app can tell whether Tuhfa Plus is active. RevenueCat also receives an identifier Apple assigns to this app on your device (the identifier for vendor), your device model, your language settings and your App Store country. We do not receive your payment details.

Error reports (off unless you turn them on)

When on, the app sends a fixed error code, the app version and the build number to our error-reporting provider, Sentry. Error reports never include cycle data, your account or a device identifier.

Analytics (off unless you turn them on)

When on, the app sends usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog, under one shared anonymous identifier. Analytics never include cycle data and are not linked to you.

Apple Health (Tuhfa Plus, optional)

If you allow it, Tuhfa reads your menstrual flow records from Apple Health and adds the ones you choose to your records on your phone. Tuhfa never writes to Apple Health. Data read from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, and it never leaves your phone: it is not included in cloud backups.

Location (optional)

If you tap "Use current location", your location is used on your phone to calculate prayer times and is stored rounded. It is not sent to us, except inside your encrypted backup.

Messages you send us (optional)

If you use Report a problem, or write to the support address, your mail app sends us your email address and your message. If you turn on Add my summary, the message also contains today's answer, the reasons for it and your madhhab; the reasons can mention a pregnancy, a birth or a pregnancy loss. Messages arrive in an inbox provided by Google (Gmail).

Network connections

Each service the app connects to (Supabase and Apple, RevenueCat when you open Tuhfa Plus, are signed in, or have Tuhfa Plus on this device, and Sentry or PostHog only if you turn them on) receives your device's IP address as part of the connection; PostHog is set to discard it and Sentry not to store it. Apart from the identifier for vendor that RevenueCat receives, the app sends no device identifier, and it never sends an advertising identifier.

What we never collect

Your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier. We do not receive your email address or your records in readable form unless you write to us.

How long we keep data

Your account and your newest three encrypted backups are kept until you delete them or delete your account; when a newer backup is made, older ones beyond the newest three are deleted. Records of account-deletion requests hold no account details and are deleted after 30 days. Records of subscription events contain no health data and are kept only as long as needed to operate the service and meet legal obligations. Our providers keep their own records under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Erase everything on your phone: Settings > Privacy and data > Delete all app data from this device.
  • Delete individual backups in Settings > Cloud backup, or your whole cloud account and every backup in Settings > Account. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
  • Turn error reports and analytics on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Share a text summary of today's answer, with the records it used, at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to processing. Because your records are encrypted on your phone, most of them can only be accessed through the app itself. To make a request, write to abouelenein.baz@gmail.com.

Children

Tuhfa is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Service providers

Apple (sign-in, purchases, Apple Health), Supabase (account and encrypted backup storage, United States), RevenueCat (subscription status), Sentry (error reports, opt-in, United States), PostHog (analytics, opt-in, United States) and Google (the support inbox, only for messages you send us).

Changes
