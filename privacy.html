Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · Publisher: Baz Abouelenein · Contact: abouelenein.baz@gmail.com

The short version

Tuhfa keeps your records on your phone, in an encrypted database. You can use every free feature without an account. Nothing about your cycle, prayers, fasts or notes is sent to us in a form we can read. We do not show ads, sell data or track you across apps and websites.

What stays on your phone

Your cycle, discharge, postpartum, prayer, fasting and Qada records, your notes, your school and settings, and the prayer location you choose (rounded to about 1 km) are stored in an encrypted database on your phone. The key is kept in the iOS Keychain. Prayer times are calculated on your phone.

What leaves your phone, and only if you choose it

Cloud account (optional). If you sign in with Apple, we receive an anonymous Apple sign-in token. We do not ask Apple for your name or email. Our backend provider, Supabase, creates an account identifier for you.

Encrypted backup (Tuhfa Plus). Before a backup leaves your phone, your records are encrypted on the phone with a key derived from a recovery passphrase that only you know. We store the encrypted file and basic details about it (your account identifier, when it was made, its size). We cannot read its contents, and we cannot recover it if you lose both your passphrase and your phone.

Subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat, our subscription provider, receives your account identifier and your purchase history from Apple so that the app can tell whether Tuhfa Plus is active. We do not receive your payment details.

Error reports (off unless you turn them on). When on, the app sends a fixed error code, the app version and the build number to our error-reporting provider, Sentry. Error reports never include cycle data or anything that identifies you.

Analytics (off unless you turn them on). When on, the app sends usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog, under one shared anonymous identifier. Analytics never include cycle data and are not linked to you.

Apple Health (Tuhfa Plus, optional). If you allow it, Tuhfa reads your menstrual flow records from Apple Health and adds the ones you choose to your records on your phone. Tuhfa never writes to Apple Health. Data read from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, and it leaves your phone only inside your encrypted backup, if you use backup.

Location (optional). If you tap "Use current location", your location is used on your phone to calculate prayer times and is stored rounded. It is not sent to us, except inside your encrypted backup.

What we never collect

Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier, and any of your records in readable form.

How long we keep data

Encrypted backups and your account are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Rate-limit records are deleted after one day. Records of subscription events and of account-deletion requests contain no health data and are kept only as long as needed to operate the service and meet legal obligations. Our providers keep their own records under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Erase everything on your phone: Settings > Delete all app data from this device.
  • Delete individual backups, or your whole cloud account and every backup: Settings > Account, backup and subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
  • Turn error reports and analytics on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Export your records as text at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to processing. Because your records are encrypted with your own passphrase, most of them can only be accessed through the app itself. To make a request, write to abouelenein.baz@gmail.com.

Children

Tuhfa is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Service providers

Apple (sign-in, purchases, Apple Health), Supabase (account and encrypted backup storage, United States), RevenueCat (subscription status), Sentry (error reports, opt-in, United States) and PostHog (analytics, opt-in, United States).

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be shown in the app.
